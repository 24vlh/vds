Forms

Advanced Form Controls

Advanced forms style multiselects, tag inputs, choice buttons/cards/chips, segmented controls, toggles, steppers, and calendar scaffolds. Custom interaction behavior is application-owned.

Source truth

Imports

Advanced forms are authored in src/components/forms-advanced.css and ship through dist/components/forms-advanced.css. They compose with core forms. 

@import "@24vlh/vds/dist/core.css";
@import "@24vlh/vds/dist/components/forms.css";
@import "@24vlh/vds/dist/components/forms-advanced.css";

Examples

Multiselect and selected chips

Runtime

Custom controls need full behavior

Advanced controls are CSS-only scaffolds. They are not a widget library.

  • Choice buttons, segmented controls, and toggles must synchronize checked/pressed state.
  • Steppers must validate numeric range and keep aria-valuenow accurate.
  • Calendar buttons need labels, selected state, keyboard navigation, and disabled dates.
  • Chip remove buttons need names such as "Remove JavaScript".
